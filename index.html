01 — Impact

The rewrite that saved $152K a month

My favorite project so far, and a good example of how I work.

Role
Designer and lead engineer
Stack
BigQuery, Dataproc, Pig, Airflow
Year
2026 · Yahoo
Outcome
−94% cost, −85% runtime

Five Pig jobs on Dataproc, replaced by three SQL queries.

Yahoo's core event pipelines ran as Pig scripts on Dataproc clusters. They worked, but they were slow and cost about $161K a month.

I mapped what each job actually produced, folded five pipelines into three partitioned BigQuery SQL jobs, and ran both versions side by side until the numbers matched.

The new jobs cost about $9K a month and finish 85% faster. The design doc I wrote became the template for the migrations that followed.
